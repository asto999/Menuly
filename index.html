What's probably happening right now

  • A table scans the code, reads the menu, then spends five minutes waving someone down just to place the order.
  • At peak hours, orders get relayed from memory or shouted across the kitchen — and mistakes follow.
  • A guest who wanted a drink or dessert doesn't bother, because it means flagging someone down all over again.
  • By the time an order reaches the kitchen, the table's patience — and the upsell — is already gone.

What Menuly actually does

ITM-01

One scan, start to finish

The same QR code already on the table takes guests straight into a live menu they can browse and order from — no app to download, no account to create.

ITM-02

Orders land in the kitchen instantly

The moment a guest taps send, a ticket appears on the kitchen screen with the table number, items, and notes — no relay, no mishearing.

ITM-03

The menu updates itself

Sold out of something at 9pm? Mark it from the kitchen screen and it disappears from every table's menu immediately — no more promising dishes that don't exist.

ITM-04

Easy upsells, no awkward ask

A dessert or drink suggestion appears right when they're deciding, instead of relying on a server to remember to mention it.

How it actually gets built

  1. 1

  2. 2

    We set it up on your existing codes

    No new QR codes to print — Menuly points your current ones at the live menu instead of a PDF.

  3. 3

    It goes live

    Usually about a week, start to finish — and the menu stays editable by you from then on.
